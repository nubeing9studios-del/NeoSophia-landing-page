What Is Coherence?

Coherence is the alignment between what you intend, what you feel, and how you act. When all three move in the same direction, clearer thinking, calmer communication, and more intentional action become possible.

Signal Capture: The Primary Entry Tool

Signal Capture is used when you know something needs to happen but cannot clearly identify what it is. It turns unclear internal signal into one clear, actionable next step.

The Coherence Loop

A simple four-phase awareness cycle that can be used in moments of internal disruption. It does not require analysis. It requires practice.

Phase 1

Recognition

Notice internal activation or emotional shift.

Phase 2

Stabilisation

Pause, slow breathing, reduce immediate reaction.

Phase 3

Alignment

Ask: what outcome do I want to create here?

Phase 4

Action

Choose behaviour consistent with that outcome.
